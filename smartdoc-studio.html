Cards are placed at true physical size for printing. In your browser's print dialog, set scale to "Actual size / 100%" and turn off headers and footers for accurate results.

Automatic detection first tries OpenCV edge/contour detection plus a colour-based method, and shows whichever result best matches the card size you picked above. In the background it then also tries a small trained AI corner-detection model (loaded once, lazily, from a public CDN) and — only if that model is confident and the shape makes sense — swaps in its result a moment later, so the corners may visibly improve a second or two after you upload or capture. None of this is a dedicated ID-template detector and the AI model needs an internet connection the first time it loads, so on a cluttered background treat the result as a starting guess, not a guarantee — always check and, if needed, drag the four dots onto the card's own corners before tapping "Crop this side". A plain, uncluttered background behind the card (a sheet of white paper, for example) still gives the most reliable result. Enhancement never changes the card's printed text or numbers.

A signature made here is a convenience mark (drawn, typed, or cut from a photo) — it is not a cryptographic or legally-verified e-signature, so check what your document or process actually requires before relying on it. Background removal is a simple brightness threshold, not a perfect cut-out.

Corrections are drawn on top of the page; your original file is never changed and stays downloadable separately. Blackout, whiteout and custom mask permanently cover the selected area only in the exported copy. "Edit text" works only on computer-generated (non-scanned) PDF pages: tap existing text to select it, retype it, and tick "Cover original" to paint over the old text with the cover colour before the new text is drawn — this is a position-matched overlay, not a true rewrite of the PDF's internal text, and detected position, size and colour are approximate. Bengali/Hindi typed text isn't supported yet — Latin letters only.

Everything runs in your browser; files are not uploaded anywhere. This tool scans, crops, annotates and converts documents. It does not alter or recreate identity details on official documents.
