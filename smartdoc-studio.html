SmartDoc Studio

Drag the four corner dots onto the document corners, then tap Crop.

Corrections are drawn on top of the page; your original file is never changed and stays downloadable separately. Blackout, whiteout and custom mask permanently cover the selected area only in the exported copy. "Edit text" works only on computer-generated (non-scanned) PDF pages: tap existing text to select it, retype it, and tick "Cover original" to paint over the old text with the cover colour before the new text is drawn — this is a position-matched overlay, not a true rewrite of the PDF's internal text, and detected position, size and colour are approximate. Bengali/Hindi typed text isn't supported yet — Latin letters only.

Everything runs in your browser; files are not uploaded anywhere. This tool scans, crops, annotates and converts documents. It does not alter or recreate identity details on official documents.
